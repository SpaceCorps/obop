**Timing tests.** The tests in `crates/obop/src/sso_tests.rs` that pin the limit of a call and the eight places for waiting do not wait for a guess of how long a busy machine takes. The stand-in holds a request until the test lets go of it (`Idp::hold`, `Idp::release`), the server tells a test how many requests wait (`HttpState::sso_waiting_for_test`), and a test lets go of the fetch it holds when the thing it pins has happened: eight wait and the ninth has been told; eight exchanges are in the air and the late fetch was refused. The limit is shortened while the exchange before the call under test is in the air, so that it is the limit of that call alone, and the test world runs with a 30 s limit unless a test sets its own. Before this, on a machine at 99 percent CPU with other builds running (32 logical processors), 3 of 8 runs of `cargo test -p obop --bin obop sso_tests` failed, and the first run of the next series failed in five tests; after, 12 of 12 runs passed, and 6 of 6 more with 64 spinning processes added (`3.2 s` to `7.0 s` per run).
